## What happened

Gauthier's nine‑game run began on Oct. 29 and has produced 16 goals, the most by any active player in that span. He has led the NHL in goals, hat tricks, multigoal games, even‑strength and power‑play goals, and game‑winning goals for the season.

He is the first player since Alexander Mogilny (1992‑93) to record three hat tricks in a five‑game span, and the first to do so within an eight‑day period. The streak also puts him on track to match the 20‑goal record for a calendar month set by Selanne and Joe Malone, if he scores six more goals in November.

## Why it matters

The streak demonstrates how advanced metrics can reveal a player's true impact beyond raw goal totals. For the Ducks, Gauthier's hot streak could shift playoff positioning and give the team a potent offensive weapon. For the league, it underscores the importance of early‑season momentum and the role of shot speed and shot quality in goal production.

## How to think about it

When evaluating a hot streak, consider the underlying metrics that sustain it—shot speed, shot quality, and conversion rate—rather than just raw goal totals. Track the player's performance over a larger sample to assess consistency, and compare it to historical benchmarks for similar streaks.
