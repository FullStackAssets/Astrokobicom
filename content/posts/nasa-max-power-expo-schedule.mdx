NASA is gearing up for MAX POWER, the agency’s newest aerospace expo and air show slated for Nov. 6‑8 at Kennedy Space Center. The three‑day event will feature the quiet supersonic X‑59 aircraft, Moon Base updates, and a lineup of autonomous vehicles and lunar rovers from NASA and partners. With live news conferences and public access, the expo aims to bring cutting‑edge space and aviation technology to a broader audience. For enthusiasts and professionals alike, MAX POWER offers a rare glimpse of the future of air and space travel.

<Callout type="takeaway">MAX POWER will spotlight NASA’s latest supersonic aircraft, lunar outpost plans, and a host of cutting‑edge aerospace demonstrations.</Callout>

## What happened
NASA released a detailed schedule for MAX POWER on Friday, Nov. 6, running through Sunday, Nov. 8. The program includes a 10 a.m. nuclear NASA media gaggle, a 2‑4 p.m. media preview, and a 5 p.m. news conference on Nov. 7 that will deliver the agency’s latest Moon Base update.

Public gates open at 9 a.m. EST on Nov. 7 and Nov. 8, offering family‑friendly activities, meet‑and‑greets with astronauts, engineers, and leadership, and live streams of the air show. Highlights include an opening air‑show remark by Deputy Administrator Matt Anderson, an F‑5 flyover featuring Administrator Jared Isaacman, and a showcase of the X‑59 quiet supersonic plane.

In addition to the air show, external collaborators will present autonomous vehicles, Moon rovers, and other technologies that illustrate the next generation of space exploration.

## Why it matters
MAX POWER serves as a public‑facing bridge between NASA’s cutting‑edge research and everyday audiences, reinforcing support for ambitious programs like the lunar South‑Pole outpost. By displaying the X‑59, NASA demonstrates progress toward quieter supersonic travel, a technology that could reshape commercial aviation. The event also catalyzes industry partnerships, giving private innovators a platform to align with NASA’s strategic goals and accelerate technology transfer.
