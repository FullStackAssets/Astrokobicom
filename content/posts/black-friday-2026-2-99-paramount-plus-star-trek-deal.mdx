Black Friday 2026 just landed a surprise for Star Trek fans: Paramount+ is offering its Essential and Premium plans for only $2.99 a month for the first two months. The promotion gives instant access to 12 original series and 14 movies, covering everything from the original 1966 episodes to the latest season of Strange New Worlds. The deal runs through November 30, after which the price jumps to $8.99 for Essential and $13.99 for Premium. Fans can cancel anytime, but the two‑month window is the only chance to watch the entire franchise at a fraction of the usual cost.

<Callout type="takeaway">For two months, Star Trek fans can binge the entire franchise on Paramount+ for just $2.99 a month.</Callout>

## What happened
Paramount+ rolled out the promotion on November 1, coinciding with the 60th anniversary of the first Star Trek episode, "The Man Trap". The offer applies to both Essential (ad‑supported) and Premium (ad‑free) tiers, with the same discounted rate for the initial period.

Subscribers receive access to every season of Star Trek, including the original series, The Next Generation, Deep Space Nine, Voyager, Enterprise, Discovery, Picard, Strange New Worlds, and the newest Starfleet Academy. The catalog also contains 14 original films, except for occasional licensing rotations like Star Trek: Prodigy.

## Why it matters
The promotion lowers the barrier for new fans to explore the franchise’s history and gives long‑time Trekkies a cost‑effective way to revisit older seasons. It also positions Paramount+ as a competitive streaming platform during a crowded holiday season, potentially driving long‑term subscriptions. However, the steep price hike after the two‑month period may deter those who plan to stay beyond the promotion.

## How to think about it
When deciding whether to take advantage of the deal, compare the discounted rate to the standard monthly price and calculate how many episodes you plan to watch. If you only need a few seasons, the two‑month window may be sufficient; if you want to binge the entire franchise, consider whether the price jump after the promotion is acceptable. Also check device compatibility and whether you prefer ad‑free viewing before committing.

<Callout type="warning">After the two‑month period, the price will rise to $8.99 for Essential and $13.99 for Premium; cancel before the deadline if you want to avoid the increase.</Callout>
