Samsung announced that its native News app, widget, and left‑most home‑screen feed will disappear from Galaxy phones and tablets on December 21 2026. The service, launched in 2023, delivered daily headlines, sports scores, and stock updates directly on the device. Users in India and South Korea already see a discontinuation notice inside the app. The shutdown means the app will be removed and all associated user data will be erased, except where local law mandates retention.

<Callout type="takeaway">Samsung is ending its built‑in News service on Galaxy devices, deleting user data on Dec 21 2026.</Callout>

## What happened
The Samsung News app debuted in 2023 as a pre‑installed offering that aggregated news, sports, and market data without needing a separate browser. It also provided a widget and a dedicated media page on the leftmost home‑screen slot.

A discontinuation notice posted inside the app for India and South Korea states that the service will cease operation at 00:00 UTC on December 21 2026. After that date the app, its widget, and the home‑screen feed will be removed from all supported Galaxy phones and tablets.

Samsung says user profiles, personalization settings, and saved articles will be deleted, unless local regulations require temporary retention. The company has not announced a direct replacement for the integrated news experience.

## Why it matters
The removal strips Galaxy devices of a native, always‑on news source, pushing users toward third‑party apps and potentially increasing data usage. It also reflects Samsung’s broader strategy of phasing out bundled services that see limited engagement. For power users who relied on the feed for quick updates, the loss may affect daily workflows and device storage expectations.

## How to think about it
Before the shutdown, export any saved articles or take screenshots of important information. Consider installing a reputable news aggregator from the Play Store and customizing widgets to replicate the previous layout. Review your home‑screen organization to fill the vacant leftmost slot with a tool that matches your workflow.

<Callout type="warning">Do not assume your saved news items will be retained; back them up now to avoid permanent loss.</Callout>
